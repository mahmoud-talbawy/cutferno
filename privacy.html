Privacy Policy

Cutferno Uploader · Last updated: 2026-10-29

What this app is

Cutferno Uploader is a private, single-operator tool. It is used only by its operator to upload videos to, and read basic data of, the Cutferno YouTube channel that the operator owns. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access and store

  • The app accesses only the operator's own YouTube channel: it uploads videos and reads basic channel and video information (such as titles and view counts).
  • The app does not collect, store, sell or share any data from other people or viewers.
  • The OAuth tokens that allow the app to act on the channel are stored only on the operator's private server. They are never shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used on this website.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
